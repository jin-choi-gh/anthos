# Anthos

Anthos is a web and mobile app where users build, grow and enjoy collections of poems. 

The project is rooted in the image of the anthology —  a word derived from the Ancient Greek ἀνθολογέω (*anthologéō*, “I gather flowers”), from *ánthos* (“flower”) and *légō* (“I gather, pick, collect”). Anthos embeds the “I” back into collecting poetry: every user is a gatherer-of-flowers, arranging and cultivating poems into anthologies to suit their state of mind or personal taste, or to mark a particular occasion. 

Anthologists will be able to choose from tens of thousands of poems (eventually hundreds of thousands in many languages), and search across themes, genres, moods, images, symbols, styles, movements, occasions, forms, lengths, dates, geographies, idioms, identities, authors, and particular words and phrases. A short anthology may be akin to a bouquet; a longer one to a botanical garden or a jungle. Some anthologies may be fixed, others flow with the seasons.  

Users will be able to introduce, annotate and share their anthologies, as well as following the public collections of others. The app aims to nurture a diverse community of poem-gatherers, bringing the playlist culture to poetry.
